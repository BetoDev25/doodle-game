🎨 Welcome to Doodle Duel!

Doodle Duel is a fun drawing game where you and another player take turns doodling and finishing each other's drawings. Here's how it works:

1

You and an opponent each have 10 seconds to draw a quick doodle.

2

You swap doodles and have 60 seconds to finish your opponent's drawing.

3

Both drawings are displayed side by side at the end.
