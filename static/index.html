Welcome to Sketch Switch!

Sketch Switch is a drawing game where you and a random partner finish each other's drawing. Here's how it works:

1

You and your partner each have 10 seconds to draw a quick doodle.

2

You swap doodles and have 60 seconds to finish each other's drawing.

3

Both drawings are displayed side by side at the end.
